- T1-REVIEW-FOLLOWUP-ISSUE-2: the closure step `followups` records the GitHub issue of the advisory and deferred findings of a merged card. `aidlc card close --followups`, which `--all` includes, finds the issue by its marker or submits it, at most once per run; `--skip-followups "<why>"` records the step with no issue. `CardRun` gains `closure.followups`, `followupIssue`, `followupNote` and `followupPending`; a closure stored before the step has it done. Every write of `aidlc card close` merges its changes into the stored run under the card-run lock and the card lease lock.
