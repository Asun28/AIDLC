- T1-CONTINUE-REVIEW: `CardRunner.continueStop` continues a card run stopped for review on the same card in one locked update: it applies `continueReviewStop` to the record read inside the lock, writes the continuation, the lifted ledger, no stop, no blocker and the state BUILD, keeps every counter, finding, invocation outcome, effort, CI, candidate, receipt and deadline, and owes one `CARD_STATE` event from STOP to `readmitted` that carries the continuation and that the next `card next` delivers when its append failed; a refusal, or a card with no run, writes and appends nothing. While a continuation admits a count, the R3 allowance, the `reviewExhausted` evidence, the second pass (count minus 1), the review directive and the reviewer prompt read it in place of the two- and four-decision allowances, and the gate stop and the admission refusal name the continuation. The command `aidlc card continue` arrives with T1-CONTINUE-GOAL.
