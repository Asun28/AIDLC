- T1-TEST-CLI-INPROC-2 (tests): `runCliInProcess` in `tests/infra/cli-inprocess.ts` runs `main` of `src/cli/main.ts` in the test process and returns the status, stdout and stderr that `bin/aidlc.js` returns. The call gets the environment a child would (on win32, of keys equal apart from case the first in sorted order). Each call starts with no cached session actor or repository identity (`resetRepoIdentityForTests`, new in `src/state/paths.ts`, which also drops a pending prefetch), runs and removes the exit listeners it adds, and collects output written with `fs.writeSync` to fd 1 or 2 and by `spawnSync` children with inherited stdio; cwd, env, exitCode, `process.exit`, stdin, the stream writers and the caller's actor are restored after it.
