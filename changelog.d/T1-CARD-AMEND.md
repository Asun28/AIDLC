- T1-CARD-AMEND: `CardRunner.amendCard` records the amendment of a started card on its run (who, why, the next card revision, the changed contract fields and the evidence they invalidate) and keeps its findings, review counters and stop: a change to `acceptance`, `dod_command`, `dod_exit` or `tdd` drops the DoD receipt and reopens the effort episode, and a change to `requirements`, `acceptance`, `allow_paths`, `forbid` or `non_goals` gives the candidate a new digest, so R3 reviews it again; the command arrives with T1-AMEND-COMMAND.
