- T1-CONTINUE-COMMAND: `aidlc card continue <cardId> --by <who> --reason <text> [--decisions <n>] [--goal <id>]` records the owner's continuation of a card run stopped for review through `GoalController.continueCard`: the stop is lifted with up to 4 further R3 decisions, a goal stopped for that stop is re-admitted, and the output gives the run's state, the continuation, whether this call recorded it and re-admitted the goal, and the next command (`aidlc goal extend` first when the run's deadline has passed).
