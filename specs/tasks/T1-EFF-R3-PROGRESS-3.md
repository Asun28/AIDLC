## Deliverable

With a command reviewer, R3 stops a card for a human ruling only when a block from decision 2 on re-raises a finding that the previous R3 decision raised or the author disputed and that is still unresolved, both fixed when the decision was dispatched, or when decision 4 blocks; the stop is recorded on the decision with its detail, so a superseded commit still stops the card and no further decision is admitted; any other merge-blocking block returns the card to REVIEW_FIX, and its repaired candidate restarts the R2 cycle and gets the next decision. The ship-path reviewer, the base-sync decision, a review needed after two decisions whose last passed, the no-verdict retry, the deadlines and R2 stay as they are, and every statement of the second-block rule says the new one.
