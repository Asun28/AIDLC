# T1-OUTCOME-REPLAY

## Deliverable
`evals/review/replay/` holds confirmed bug cases made from the findings that both models or the owner labelled `true-defect` in `bench/outcome/labels-final.jsonl`, and no clean case. `bench/outcome/replay-main.json` holds one formal bench run of main's reviewer over them (its recall is the recall baseline), three trials of ten of them with whether each case's three bench outcomes agree (`agree`) and whether its three reviewer verdicts agree (`verdictAgree`), and the labelled findings left out (`leftOut`).

## Why
Spec R4 of specs/reliability-baseline-2.md, as the owner set it on 2026-11-15 (intent/loop-evidence.md, Decisions after the spot check): a recall baseline from the true defects the owner and both models stand behind. The adjudicator's labels are not used, since the owner disagreed with 17 of its 20 sampled labels, and there are no clean cases, since too few candidates have no true-defect finding for a false-block rate. A later change to what R3 may block on must keep recall on this set.
