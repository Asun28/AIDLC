# T0-RELEASE-OP-REISSUE

## Deliverable
A release deploy or recovery whose operation the ledger already holds is never issued a second time. An operation without a recorded outcome makes `aidlc release next` wait, naming `aidlc ops reconcile <id> --status <s>`, and stop for time after the reconciliation grace. A recorded outcome the attempt missed becomes the step. Two callers of one step record one intent and run the command once.

## Why
Audit of 2026-11-11, items F7 and N1, confirmed by two models; card reviewed by two more on 2026-11-11 and amended to the planner's rulings. The rule of the loop is "UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN, reconcile first". Adding `intended` to the reconcile filter alone is not enough: an `intended` record has no provider id, so the lookup skips it, and after a human records its outcome the step is still absent from the attempt and `issue` runs again. The step's idempotency key is the field that ties a ledger record to the step.
