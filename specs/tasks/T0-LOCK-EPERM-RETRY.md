# T0-LOCK-EPERM-RETRY

## Deliverable
A lock acquisition of `updateJson` retries an exclusive create of the lock that is refused with EPERM while the lock path is absent (Windows, a lock of that name being deleted by another process), after the loop's 10 ms wait, at most twice; the third such refusal, or one after whose wait the deadline has passed, propagates as that EPERM, never `LOCKED`. Every other error of the create propagates at once as today, a held lock still ends in `locked(file, lock)` at the deadline, and `createExclusive` and its other callers do not change.

## Why
Audit of 2026-11-11, item F3; two consultants analysed it and the coordinator ruled on 2026-11-12 for a bounded retry in the lock acquisition of `updateJson` only, with an allowance of three refusals per acquisition that a held lock does not give back. The refusal is a transient of Windows file deletion: a refusal can fail a command, and some journal sites then need operator recovery.

## Planner rulings of 2026-11-12 (after the pre-review of e1fb2054)

A Codex pre-review of the candidate e1fb2054 found the loop correct in every path and two points to change. Where a detail below differs from these rulings, the ruling holds. No requirement changes but the parenthetical of R6 (f).

1. R6 (f): the parenthetical added to the T0-JOURNAL-APPEND-LOCK entry of CHANGELOG.md names the deadline case too: "(since T0-LOCK-EPERM-RETRY: after the third such refusal of one acquisition, or after an earlier one whose wait reaches the deadline)".
