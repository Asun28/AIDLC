# T1-REVIEW-RESPONSES

## Deliverable
In this repository 1 of 464 recorded findings was disputed, and a dispute is the only response a card run records, so nothing says how many reviewer findings the author judged wrong, intentional or not worth fixing, and reviewer precision is unknown. This card records that judgement as a kind on each dispute (`aidlc review dispute <card> <id> --note "<why>" --kind wrong|intentional|not-worth-fixing`, default `wrong`), carries it on the `FINDING_DISPUTED` event and on the `review findings` line, and reports every finding of a card by response in `aidlc review stats`: fixed, wrong, intentional, not-worth-fixing, open, in the JSON (`findings.responses`) and in the line form. It implements spec R13, the responses half of R16, and R21 for its own docs.

Decisions. The kind is optional with no zod default: a record written before this card is not rewritten with a kind its author never chose, and every reader reads an absent kind as `wrong`, the only response the command could record before. `--kind` is parsed by `parseDisputeKind` (exported from `src/core/review-policy.ts`) at the CLI boundary, before `cardCtx` reads or creates the run, so a refused kind writes nothing; `CardRunner.disputeFinding` and `disputeFinding` take a typed `DisputeKind` that defaults to `wrong`. `fixed` is a finding resolved with an open disposition, not one that was never disputed: a finding disputed, then re-raised or withdrawn, then repaired, was fixed; it also counts a finding that a later round of its stage dropped without a repair, which OPERATIONS states. The five responses partition `total`; `responses.open` equals `open`, the three kinds sum to `disputed` plus the findings resolved while disputed, and `open + disputed + resolved === total` holds unchanged.

Split. Plan item 5 also names the `[pre-existing]` tag. Both halves together were estimated at about 650 net lines (source about 170, tests about 320, docs about 15, plus the overrun the earlier review cards measured against their budgets), above a 3-hour card. The tag, its changed-line check on the command path (`finalizeReview` to `enforceCitations` in `src/review/pre-review.ts`, reached from `CardRunner.preReview` and `CardRunner.formalReview`) and on the ship path (`stripAdvisoryTags` in `CardRunner.applyShipResult`, `src/loop/card-runner.ts:2219`), the REVIEW.md and prompt text, the `reviewPolicyVersion` bump and the `preExistingAdvisory` count go to the follow-up card T1-REVIEW-PRE-EXISTING, which the plan's order of work needs after this card.

Composition. Line numbers are from main at 597e9a2. The loop-efficiency goal (T1-EFF-R3-PROGRESS, T1-EFF-R2-ANGLE-FALLBACK) and T1-REVIEW-CLASS-SWEEP change `src/core/types.ts`, `src/core/review-policy.ts`, `src/loop/card-runner.ts` and `src/review/stats.ts` before this card; re-read them at PREPARE, keep the fields they add (for example `ReviewFinding.class`) and add the dispute kind beside them.
