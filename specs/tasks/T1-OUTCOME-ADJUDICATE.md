# T1-OUTCOME-ADJUDICATE

## Deliverable
`node bench/outcome/adjudicate.ts` takes each row of `bench/outcome/labels.jsonl` whose two model labels differ and that no ruling settled, asks Claude Opus 5.5 at high effort, in a read-only checkout of the reviewed commit, for one of the five labels given the finding, the cited code and both labellers' answers, keeps every call in a local log with its cost, and stops at 35 USD. A sample of 20 of its labels, fixed by a seed, goes to the owner on a sheet that hides its label. The script writes `bench/outcome/labels-final.jsonl` (every row with its final label and where it came from) and `bench/outcome/adjudication.json` (counts, spend, and the owner's agreement with the adjudicator on the sample).

## Why
Owner decisions of 2026-11-14: about 170 disagreements are too many to rule by hand, so a third call settles them, and the owner's labels on a random 20 measure its error rate, which T1-OUTCOME-REPORT states. T1-OUTCOME-REPLAY reads `final` from labels-final.jsonl.

Labeller B gave <labelB>. The end of its answer:
