# T0-AUDIT-BINDING

## Deliverable
`aidlc audit verify` gives `independently-verified` only to a goal whose sealed manifest names a final candidate digest and whose evidence of the five candidate-bound kinds each names a candidate digest; the two new findings are warnings, so the exit code does not change for them. Evidence retained for a card is compared with the candidate digests of that card's succeeded merges in the journal, and other evidence with the final digest the caller supplies (the CLI supplies the manifest's). `aidlc audit seal` requires a non-blank `--final-digest` and refuses, inside its lock section and before it journals anything, a manifest that holds unbound evidence. The loop's retain suggestions name `--candidate`.

## Why
Audit of 2026-11-11, item C10; two consultants analysed it and the coordinator ruled on 2026-11-12, then ruled on two Codex reviews of this card. The level claims evidence bound to the final candidate, but nothing required a binding: a read-only scan of this repository's state on 2026-11-12 found 493 of 711 entries of the five kinds with no candidate digest and 11 of 19 sealed manifests with no final digest, all written before the binding existed. The ruling makes the two new findings warnings that withhold the level, since a blocking finding would turn `audit verify` red for almost every past goal, and makes the seal the gate for new goals. Evidence bound to another candidate than the one merged is a true finding, also in an unsealed manifest.
