# T0-GOAL-CLOSE-LEASE-2

## Deliverable
`GoalController.nextInClose` (src/loop/controller.ts) settles the goal lease before the goal is saved DONE. The closing session's own lease is released at its generation, as before. A lease another session left to expire, the only foreign lease `next()` lets a session close under, is taken over after the goal's operations are reconciled, journaled as `GOAL_TAKEOVER` with the previous owner, and released. An absent or released lease is left as it is. A refused takeover throws before anything is saved, so the goal stays CLOSE. After R3 decision 1 every decision is taken on the record under the lease lock, the takeover journals a handoff intent before its lease write, and the release journals `GOAL_TAKEOVER` from that intent when it is missing, so a close interrupted between the takeover and the release is completed by the next call with one `GOAL_TAKEOVER`. DONE, `GOAL_DONE`, the board and the `done` directive therefore always come together. After R3 decision 2, a close that takes over a lease whose own close takeover was left without its `GOAL_TAKEOVER` (its taker failed at that append and never called again) journals that event first, so the earlier handoff is not lost when a later session finishes the goal. Until now a goal closed by a session other than the intake session threw FencedError after DONE was saved. This card carries T0-GOAL-CLOSE-LEASE by merge at 9a4afdf and adds the R7 completion. docs/OPERATIONS.md, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and CHANGELOG.md state the rule.
