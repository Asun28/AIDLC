# T0-OP-INTENT-DUP

## Deliverable
`CardRunner.ship` (src/loop/card-runner.ts) continues under the `intended` merge intent that an earlier ship of the same candidate left behind, instead of recording a second intent beside it. It appends `OPERATION_INTENT` for that intent only when the journal names none, and its two waits never cancel an intent they took over. After a ship fails between its intent and its dispatch, the next ship merges under the one intent, and no `intended` operation is left to hold the goal at `wait` on `reconcile:<id>`, refuse a takeover or block `aidlc audit verify`. docs/OPERATIONS.md, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and CHANGELOG.md state the rule.

## Design

### Write order
Unchanged except for the take-over. Under the card lease lock: the fence, then the duplicate check (`ops.findDuplicate`; an `issued`, `running` or `UNKNOWN` record returns `wait` on `operation:<id>`, as at 794624a), then either the take-over of the earliest `intended` merge intent of the same goal, card, target, candidate digest and owner generation (R1) or `ops.recordIntent`. Lock released. `OPERATION_INTENT` append: always for a new intent, for a taken-over one only when the goal journal names none (R2). Then the enqueue or requeue, `REVIEW_REQUESTED`, the join wait, the admission, the admission wait, `REVIEW_ADMITTED` with the request cancelled on failure (T0-REVIEW-ADMIT-STRAND), the record re-read with its cleanups, `markIssued`, `OPERATION_ISSUED`, the dispatch. The two waits mark the intent `cancelled` only when this call recorded it (R3).

### What each failure leaves, and its recovery
- The lease section throws: nothing is recorded, as at 794624a.
- The `OPERATION_INTENT` append throws: the intent stays `intended` with no event; the next ship takes it over and appends the event once.
- The enqueue, the `REVIEW_REQUESTED` append or the admission throws: the intent stays `intended` with its event and the request queued or absent; the next ship takes it over, joins or recreates the request, and appends no second `OPERATION_INTENT`.
- The `REVIEW_ADMITTED` append throws: T0-REVIEW-ADMIT-STRAND cancels the request; the next ship takes the intent over and enqueues again.
- The process dies at any of these points: the same states as the matching throw, the same recovery.
- After `markIssued` the operation is `issued`: unchanged (H9, H10), a person's `aidlc ops reconcile`.

### Limits
- Two processes of one session in the ship of one card at once: the second may take over the intent the first is still issuing, and both continue on one record. The one admitted dispatches; the other waits without touching the intent (R3). If the recording process is the one refused admission, it still marks its intent `cancelled`, and the dispatching process's `markIssued` and result then overwrite that status, since the ledger has no compare-and-set. The final status is the dispatch's result. Outside this card (non_goals).
- An `intended` intent of another owner generation is never taken over. A takeover of the card refuses while the intent is unresolved, so that state needs a person's reconcile first, as at 794624a.
