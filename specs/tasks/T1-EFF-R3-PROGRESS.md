# T1-EFF-R3-PROGRESS

## Deliverable
With a command reviewer, R3 stops a card for a human ruling only when a block from decision 2 on re-raises a finding that an earlier R3 decision raised or the author disputed and that is still unresolved, or when decision 4 blocks; any other merge-blocking block returns the card to REVIEW_FIX, and its repaired candidate restarts the R2 cycle and gets the next decision. The ship-path reviewer, the base-sync decision, a review needed after two decisions whose last passed, the no-verdict retry, the deadlines and R2 stay as they are, and every statement of the second-block rule says the new one.

Where: `MAX_R3_DECISIONS = 4` next to `MAX_SUBSTANTIVE_REVIEW_DECISIONS` (`src/core/types.ts:1016`), which keeps its name and value because the ship-path reviewer and the base-sync trigger still read it; `repeatedFindings` and an optional last parameter of `recordReviewOutcome` that reaches `decideFromLedger` (`src/core/review-policy.ts:106-170`), passed only by `commitFormalResult` for a decision that is not base-sync and computed from `citedReasonsOf(verdict, changedPaths)`, `latest.findings` and the reservation's `seen` before the ledger call (`src/loop/card-runner.ts:1902-1908`); one private allowance helper in `CardRunner` read by `assess` (`reviewExhausted`, :519), the REVIEW_FIX narration (:634), `formalReviewGate` (:1227, :1245-1259), `formalAdmission` (:1426-1427) and the R3 prompt's `maxRounds` (:1563); `applyShipResult` (:2213-2450) does not change. The card-loop sentence `Two decisions, up to four for repairs; a later block re-raising an unresolved R3 or disputed finding => STOP/review.` is 116 bytes against the 106 it replaces, which leaves each copy at 6496 bytes; the R3 prompt's `a partial list wastes one of only two decisions` becomes `a finding left out costs a repair round and a decision`.
