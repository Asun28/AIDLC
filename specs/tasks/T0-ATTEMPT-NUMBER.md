# T0-ATTEMPT-NUMBER

## Deliverable
Every build directive names its attempt by the ladder number, the sequence number excluding not-counted records: the records before the attempt that are not `not-counted`, plus one. One helper in src/core/effort.ts computes it. Where a BUILD or review-fix narration already contains an attempt-number label, that label uses the ladder number and adds `(record <R> of this run; <K> not counted)` only when the numbers differ; with no not-counted record before the attempt every narration is byte-identical to main's. The stored `n`, the journal, the failure counts and the limits do not change.

## Why
Audit of 2026-11-11, item F17; two consultants analysed it and the coordinator ruled on 2026-11-12, then ruled on a Codex review of this card. With one not-counted record before an attempt (a quota wait, a tool outage, a disputed repair), the attempt is announced by its ladder number when it opens and by its record number while it runs, on a succeeded episode at BUILD, in a review-fix field, in the pre-review hand-back and in three ship repairs, so the same attempt reads as 3, then 2, then 3. T0-SHIP-NOTHING-REFUTED fixed the ship failure with nothing running and left the rest as a non-goal.
